Our story

Why we exist and how we think.

Too many software projects stall because requirements drift, communication breaks down or nobody owns the outcome. Akivnah was built to fix that: small senior teams, honest estimates and software that keeps working long after launch.

We work with startups, schools, service firms and enterprises across industries. Whatever the size of the project, the standard stays the same.

Today we build web and mobile platforms, move systems to the cloud, add data and AI features, and run the products we ship. We also build our own tools, including an HR and payroll portal we use ourselves.

Internal tools

HR & payroll portal

Challenge: Paper-based letters and payslips sent by email.

Approach: Role-based portal with branded, printable documents.

Result: One place for HR to issue and employees to find their records.

Customer apps

Self-service web app

Challenge: Customers phoned support for routine requests.

Approach: Secure login, request tracking and notifications.

Result: Fewer support calls and faster responses.

Cloud migration

Challenge: Ageing servers that were costly and hard to scale.

Approach: Containerised services with automated deployments.

Result: Predictable releases and lower hosting effort.

Analytics dashboard

Challenge: Reports built by hand in spreadsheets.

Approach: Automated pipelines feeding live dashboards.
